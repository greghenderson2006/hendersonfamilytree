Dorthea Reismann

Dorthea Reismann was born in Oberjossa, Hessen, Germany, on November 2, 1873.

Important Facts
November 2, 1873 Dorthea Reismann was born in Oberjossa, Hessen, Germany. She was the daughter of George Reismann and Ana Katharina Vetter.
February 14, 1895 She married Lorenz Louis Stein in Berkeley, California.
