Louis Lorenz Stein

Louis Lorenz Stein was a pharmacist, East Bay historian and archivist. He is best known for founding the Arlington Pharmacy in Kensington, California, the pharmacy museum at Columbia State Historic Park, and for donating collections of his historical materials to various California historical societies and museums.

Important Facts
August 21, 1902 Louis Lorenz Stein was born in Berkeley, California. He was the son of Dorthea Reismann and Lorenz Louis Stein Sr..
November 5, 1927 Louis married Mildred "Milly" Slater in Berkeley, California.
1928 Stein established the Arlington Pharmacy at 299 Arlington Avenue, Kensington, California.
August 18, 1929 Robert Louis Stein was born in Berkeley, California. Source: California Birth Index, 1905-1995.
April 14, 1933 Janet Ruth Stein was born in Berkeley, California. Source: California Birth Index, 1905-1995.
February 9, 1955 Stein and his wife Mildred bought the Vicente Martinez Adobe for $25,000 to save it from being razed and subdivided. It is now part of the John Muir National Historic Site.
November 11, 1996 Louis died in Berkeley, California. Source: California Death Index, 1940-1997.
