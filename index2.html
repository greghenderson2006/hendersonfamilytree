Captain Joseph Henderson (1826 – 1890) and Angelina Annetta Weaver (1833 – 1909)

Welcome to the Henderson Family Tree homepage. This website traces the family genealogy starting with Captain Joseph Henderson and his wife Angelina Annetta Weaver.

Captain Joseph Henderson was born on May 4, 1826 in Perth Amboy, New Jersey and died on February 8, 1890 in San Francisco, California. In 1849 he came to San Francisco to join the Gold Rush, but could not get up to the gold fields, because his ship was quarantined. He chose instead to become a steamship pilot and helped guide steamships through the hazardous waters of the Golden Gate and San Francisco Bay for nearly forty years.

He married Angelina A. Weaver of Brooklyn, New York in 1855 and had five children: Mary Caroline, John Weaver, William Campbell, Susan Vailes, and Joseph Duryea Henderson. Angelina died on November 8, 1909, while living with her daughter, Mary C. Newhall in Santa Barbara, California.

This website also traces the genealogy of ancillary and related families of the Hendersons, including: the Fords and Timmermans of San Francisco, and the Weavers, Gloors, Boisots, Wells, Duryeas, and Campbells from the East Coast.

The information is based on research material, letters, documents, and photographs that our family has saved for years. We wish to acknowledge the work of Donald and David Henderson, who collected much of the early genealogy for the 1953 Henderson Family Reunion.
