Robert Boyd Henderson Jr.
1900 - 1941

Robert B. Henderson Jr. was the son of Robert B. and Elizabeth E. Castell. He married Anna Mae Snell and had one child, Robert B. Henderson III.

Important Facts
May 9, 1900 Robert B. Henderson Jr. was the son of Elizabeth E. Castell and Robert B. Henderson Sr. Robert is listed under births reported in 1900, Borough of Brooklyn. Certificate Number 8516.
