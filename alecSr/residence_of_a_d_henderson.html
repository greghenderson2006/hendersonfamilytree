Residence of A. D. Henderson

Suffern, New York

History

This was the home of Alexander Dawson Henderson Sr., located at Campbell Avenue and the Nyack Turnpike (present-day Route 59) in Suffern, New York. Henderson began his career as a bookkeeper in New York City with the California Perfume Company, now known as Avon Products, and eventually rose to become the company’s vice president and treasurer. After spending several summers in Suffern, Henderson decided to make the community his permanent home and built this residence in 1906. The house stood for 45 years before it was razed in 1951.
