Portrait of Alexander D. Henderson Sr.

  • Artist: M. J. Keith (American, active mid-20th century)
  • Date: c. 1950s (posthumous; sitter lived 1868–1940)
  • Medium: Oil on canvas
  • Signature: Signed M J KEITH in dark pigment, lower right
  • Verso Markings: Stamped "Alexander Dawson Inc." (ADI-64) property stamp
  • Companion Piece: Portrait of Ella Brown Henderson by M. J. Keith (c. 1950s, oil on canvas, bearing matching "ADI" property stamp)

Historical & Commissioning Notes

Commissioned in the 1950s by Alexander D. Henderson's son, businessman and philanthropist Girard B. "Jerry" Henderson (1905–1983), to hang alongside a companion portrait of his mother, Ella Brown Henderson, at the Alexander Dawson, Inc. (ADI) business offices before beiing transferred via Farrow Smith to the author's collection.

A copy of this portrait was displayed for many years behind the desk of Alexander Dawson Henderson III in his Gold Coast Finance office in Florida. It was later passed down from father to son and remains in the family's collection.
