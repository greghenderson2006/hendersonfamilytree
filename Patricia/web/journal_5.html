1993 Art Journal

  • Artist: Patricia Ford Crass (American, 1923–2024)
  • Date: 1993
  • Medium: Watercolor and ink on paper (bound sketchbook journal)
  • Subject: Studies from life, landscapes, and workshop exercises
  • Context: Created during a period of intensive Bay Area study, including landscapes and still-life classes with Marilyn Simandle at the Santa Cruz on November 8, 1993
  • Collection: Family private collection

Journal & Artistic Notes

Throughout the 1980s, Patricia Ford Crass kept illustrated art journals filled with on-location watercolor studies, sketches, and handwritten reflections on composition and technique. Created in 1993—the same year she studied landscape and still-life drawing and painting with Marilyn Simandle and other artists in Santa Cruz—this journal remains in the family's private collection.
