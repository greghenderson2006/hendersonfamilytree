Scott Douglas Henderson

Scott Douglas Henderson was born on March 10, 1958, in Pompano Beach, Florida. He is the fourth child of Alexander D. Henderson III and Patricia Ford.

Important Facts
March 10, 1958 Born in Pompano Beach, Florida.
1960s Attended Hillsboro Country Day School in Florida.
1969 Family moved to Carmel Valley, California.
1976 Graduated from Robert Louis Stevenson School in Pebble Beach, California.
1980 Graduated from the University of Oregon.
Worked in restaurant management, photojournalism, and later in film production. Founded Gold Leaf Entertainment and later Henderson Contracting.
June 18, 2005 Scott is married an has two children.
