David Girard Henderson

David Girard Henderson was born on April 4, 1955, in San Jose, California. He was the third child of Alexander D. Henderson III and Patricia Ford. His middle name honors his great-uncle, Girard B. Henderson.

April 4, 1955 Born in San Jose, California.

Dave (age 5)
1957 The family moved from California to Florida, where he attended Hillsboro Country Day School.
1974 Graduated from Menlo-Atherton High School.
April 17, 1982 Married Marie Theresa Fanelli in Woodside, California.
1982 & 1984 Children: Katie Patricia Henderson and Kelly Marie Henderson.
Career David worked in restaurant ownership, film production, and later in business and real estate roles in California.
June 29, 2003 Married Lindsay Colleen Levin in Oakland, California.
