3532 N.E. 31st Avenue Home

Pompano Beach, Florida

History

In 1958, Alex Henderson his wife Patrcia, and family moved into a new house at 3532 N.E. 31st Avenue, Pompano Beach, Florida. The house was on the intercoastal. Alex later bought a boat with a hoist to pull the boat out of the water. The house had two bedrooms and bathroom on one side and a large living room, kitchen, and a master bathroom and bon the other side. The house was built in a mid-century modern style with a flat roof and large windows. The house was sold in 1963 when the family moved to a new home at Sea Ranch Lakes in Pompano Beach, Florida.
