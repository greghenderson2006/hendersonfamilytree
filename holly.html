Alexandra Holly Henderson

Holly was born on November 5, 1963 in Pompano Beach, Florida. She is an artist living in San Francisco with her husband and two daughters.
Important Facts
November 5, 1963
 Alexandra Holly Henderson was born in Pompano Beach, Florida at the Holy Cross Hospital. Holly is the daughter of Alexander D. Henderson III and Patricia Ford.

Click on picture
January 1969
 Her parents were divorced in Florida.
Summer 1969
 Holly, her mother and brothers moved to Carmel Valley, California.
1970
 The family moved to Dolores Street house in Carmel, California.
1971
 Holly moved with her mother and step-father to Atherton, California.
1974-1976
 Holly went to Middle School in Atherton.
Holly was on the ice skating team and went to competitions in the bay area.

Farah
1981
 She graduated from Menlo Atherton High School in Menlo Park, California.
1987

Holly graduated from the San Francisco Art Institute in San Francisco, California.

  Holly meets Bijan Fouladi in San Francisco.
November 22, 1992
 Holly and Bijan Fouladi were married at the La Playa Hotel in Carmel, California.

Flora

 Farah Fouladi was born in San Francisco, California. She was 7 pounds, six ounces.

 Flora Fouladi was born in San Francisco, California.
